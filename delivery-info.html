Delivery Information

Important information about product delivery and collection

Dear Valued Customer,

At UBENAMS Integrated, we prioritize your satisfaction and want to ensure you receive exactly what you expect. We strongly advise that you visit our showroom to personally inspect the items you wish to purchase. This gives you the opportunity to verify that the products meet your expectations and are in perfect condition before making your final decision.

Visiting our showroom also provides you with the unique opportunity to explore our complete range of products, discover new items, and experience the quality and craftsmanship that UBENAMS Integrated is known for. Our knowledgeable staff will be available to answer any questions and provide expert guidance to help you make the best choice for your needs.

However, if you still insist on us delivering the goods to your location, we shall be happy to accommodate your request.

Delivery Options

Showroom Pickup

Visit our showroom to inspect and collect your items personally (Recommended)

Company Delivery

We have professional drivers who deliver goods across Nigeria with location-based charges

Your Own Transport

You're welcome to send your own driver to pick up items from our showroom

Please note: We have a team of professional drivers who deliver goods to various locations across Nigeria. Delivery charges vary depending on your location, distance from our showroom, and the size/weight of your order. Our customer service team will provide you with an accurate delivery quote based on your specific requirements.

If you prefer to use your own transportation, that is perfectly acceptable. You can arrange for your driver to come to our showroom to pick up your purchased items at a time convenient for you. We will ensure your goods are properly packaged and ready for collection.

We are delighted that you chose to shop with us and deeply appreciate your business. We pray that you continue to do more business with UBENAMS Integrated, and we look forward to serving you for many years to come.
